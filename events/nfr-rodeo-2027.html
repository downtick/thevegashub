RODEO

WRANGLER NATIONAL FINALS RODEO (NFR) 2027

December 2–11, 2027 · Las Vegas, NV · Ticketed; all ten performances at the Thomas & Mack Center sell as individual nights or full packages

The championship finals of professional rodeo — ten nights of bareback riding, steer wrestling, team roping, saddle bronc, tie-down roping, barrel racing, and bull riding at the Thomas & Mack Center on the UNLV campus. The top fifteen contestants in each event compete for the world titles, and the whole city turns Western for the run, with pop-up marketplaces, the free Cowboy Christmas trade show at the convention center, and live country music across the Strip.

Best for: Rodeo fans, Western-lifestyle shoppers, and country-music crowds.

PLANNING NOTES
  • Performances start at 6:45 PM nightly — arrive early, because traffic around the Thomas & Mack Center and Tropicana Ave backs up fast on finals nights.
  • Cowboy Christmas, the free Western trade show at the Las Vegas Convention Center, runs daytime all ten days and is worth a full afternoon.
  • Rooms sell out for the full ten-day run earlier than almost any event except New Year's — book months ahead.
ACCESSIBILITY

The Thomas & Mack Center offers accessible seating on every level and accessible parking in the adjacent UNLV lots; request ADA seating when buying tickets.

Where to Stay for NFR

The Thomas & Mack Center sits on the UNLV campus just east of the south Strip. South Point is the longtime rodeo hub — it hosts its own Western events during NFR and runs shuttles — and the south-Strip resorts below are a few minutes away.
