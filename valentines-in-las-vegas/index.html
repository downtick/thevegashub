Valentine's Day in Las Vegas

Valentine's Day 2027 lands on a Sunday, so it's a full weekend — gondola rides, fountain-view suites, and the best couples' dinners in the country. Here's where to stay.

💞 Find Your Romantic Hotel

Valentine's Day 2027 falls on a Sunday, which turns it into a full romantic weekend — and Las Vegas is built for exactly this. Some of the best restaurants in the country sit a short walk apart, the fountains run all night, and you can go from a gondola ride to a rooftop nightcap without ever calling a car. Here's where to stay and how to plan it.

Dinner is the whole night

Vegas has more great Valentine's tables than any city in the country, and most of them are inside a 15-minute walk of each other. The marquee rooms — fine dining at the Bellagio, the chef's counters at the Cosmopolitan, Wynn's restaurant row, the steakhouses at Caesars — all run Valentine's menus, and they fill weeks ahead. Book the table before you book anything else.

Want the view with the meal? A fountain-view table at the Bellagio, the Eiffel Tower restaurant at Paris, or a Strip-view perch at the Cosmopolitan turns dinner into the main event. Reserve the early seating and catch the late fountain show after.

The romantic classics still work

The gondola rides at the Venetian — indoor along the canal or outdoor on the lagoon — are exactly as corny and exactly as good as you'd hope. The High Roller's Happy Half Hour cabin gives you 30 minutes over the Strip with the lights below. The couples' spas at Wynn, Aria, and the Waldorf are the quiet move for the afternoon before dinner. And the free Bellagio fountains, with the right song, are still the best no-reservation date in the city.
